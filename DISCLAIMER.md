## How this software works

The bot observes **only on-screen pixels** (like a human player) and sends
standard touch inputs through the operating system. It does **not** modify
game files, read memory, intercept network traffic, or access hidden game state.

That technical approach does **not** make live automation acceptable. Using
this software to play on official Brawl Stars servers — even in Solo Showdown
— is very likely a violation of [Supercell's Terms of Service](https://supercell.com/en/terms-of-service/)
and may be treated as botting regardless of how inputs are generated.
